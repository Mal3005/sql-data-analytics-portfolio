# Website Acquisition Analysis – Project Insights

## 1. Project Overview

This project analyses website traffic, user engagement and revenue using SQL and MySQL Workbench.

The analysis focuses on acquisition channels, device categories, geographic performance, visitor behaviour and conversion trends.

## 2. Key Findings

### 2.1 Website Acquisition by Channel

The analysis identified the following results:

| Channel | Sessions | Conversions | Revenue |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Affiliates | 779 | 1 | 12.99 |
| Direct | 20,722 | 514 | 110,232.21 |
| Display | 2,005 | 34 | 4,728.45 |
| Organic Search | 39,993 | 865 | 78,203.51 |
| Paid Search | 6,839 | 158 | 13,969.40 |
| Referral | 21,733 | 1,428 | 16,333.58 |
| Social | 7,075 | 12 | 817.38 |

**Key insights:**
- Organic Search generated the highest number of sessions.
- Direct traffic generated the highest revenue.
- Referral traffic generated the highest number of conversions.
- Social traffic had relatively low conversion performance.

### 2.2 Device Performance

| Device | Sessions | Session Share | Revenue | Revenue Share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Desktop | 71,229 | 71.88% | 350,766.28 | 94.01% |
| Mobile | 24,215 | 24.44% | 18,957.89 | 5.08% |
| Tablet | 3,647 | 3.68% | 3,377.35 | 0.91% |

**Key insights:**
- Desktop devices generated the majority of website traffic.
- Desktop users contributed 94.01% of total revenue.
- Mobile traffic represented almost one-quarter of sessions but generated only 5.08% of revenue.
- Mobile user experience and conversion optimisation could be important areas for improvement.

### 2.3 New vs Returning Visitors

| Visitor Type | Visitors | Percentage |
|---|---:|---:|
| New Visitors | 64,487 | 78.95% |
| Returning Visitors | 17,191 | 21.05% |

**Key insights:**
- Most visitors were new users.
- Returning visitors represented 21.05% of the audience.
- Retention strategies could help increase repeat visits and customer engagement.

### 2.5 Conversion Performance by Weekday

| Weekday | Sessions | Conversions | Conversion Rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Tuesday | 17,309 | 517 | 2.99% |
| Wednesday | 16,202 | 495 | 3.06% |

**Key insights:**
- Monday achieved the highest conversion rate at 3.51%.
- Wednesday achieved a conversion rate of 3.06%.
- Weekend conversion rates were lower than weekday rates.
- Monday may be a promising day for targeted marketing campaigns.

## 3. Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations can be considered:

1. **Improve mobile conversions:** Investigate the gap between mobile traffic and mobile revenue.
2. **Maintain organic search performance:** Organic Search is an important source of website traffic.
3. **Analyse referral traffic:** Investigate why referral traffic generates a high number of conversions.
4. **Improve visitor retention:** Develop strategies to encourage new visitors to return.
5. **Optimise campaign timing:** Consider testing marketing campaigns on Mondays, when conversion rates are highest.
